# Source-grounded semantic story review, v0.24.0

This review asks whether PaperStory's tree-shaped companions preserve the source's meaning and explain a coherent research story. It applies nature-reader grounding and three nature-reviewer emphasis lenses, all performed by one agent. It is not Nature certification, three independent referees, a model-generalization benchmark or a human comprehension study.

The four v0.23 readers for observational education, evidence synthesis, a mathematical proof and conceptual philosophy were compared with their original papers. The audit found 11 concrete issues: important results omitted from the trunk, missing transitions, normalized-object notation, an underexplained proof step, mixed conceptual levels and branches about the software/template instead of the paper.

Corrections restore the observed teaching-practice contrast and demographic follow-up, actual meta-analysis subgroup results and the authors' cross-field conclusion, a consistent normalized polynomial and complete local inequality, and both routes of the philosophical stronger argument. Original scope qualifications and source conflicts remain visible. The revised argument graphs use the reviewed claims and explanatory destinations.

The Skill now includes [a semantic story audit](../references/en/story-quality.md): map each central source question/result to its reading location, give reasons for major omissions, read the trunk with all branches closed, define load-bearing terms before using them, close the opening question and require each branch to deepen this paper. Exact numbers and functioning navigation are necessary but do not demonstrate that the argument has been explained.

Earlier CAHS, LoRA, Dsup and RMSNorm examples received limited source checks on selected central chains. They were not all re-audited sentence by sentence; the hydrogel paper was not subjected to a new full semantic review here. Earlier inputs, reviews and readers remain preserved in the maintenance task folder.

The detailed three-report package, source-to-revision map, corrected interactive readers and validation evidence are local task artifacts. New scientific sources are not distributed inside the installable Skill. The v0.24.0 release includes the reusable workflow and code changes; detailed local reader artifacts and new source papers are not included in the installable package.
