# Last source check

Review all decisive judgments in the thesis, trunk, walkthroughs, captions, branches, graph nodes/arrows and conclusion against original passages and actual figures. Do not limit the pass to numerals.

- Is the correct object/state being described: precursor, intermediate, product, treated specimen, dry image, hydrated material or model output?
- Does each comparison preserve variables, units, denominator, normalization, irradiation/loading history and reference treatment? Are simultaneously changing conditions visible to the reader?
- Does each panel contain the claimed metric? Read axes, legend, scale and specimen labels directly, not from a generic caption. Preserve a complete source view.
- Are identity, conversion, isolated yield, selectivity and functional response kept distinct? Are arbitrary spectral scaling and incompatible extinction coefficients respected?
- Is an author interpretation attributed, a computed result labeled, and an application prospect separated from demonstrated function? Do indirect measurements remain indirect?
- Is a stage-specific benefit being expanded to the entire synthetic route or all operating conditions?
- Does SI supply a needed control or identity check at the right point? Are unviewed attachments distinguished from absent data?
- If a source contains an inconsistency, is the discrepancy and resulting limit disclosed rather than silently repaired?
- Did every prose revision propagate to the thesis, captions, branch questions, graph findings and arrows? Recheck the source before updating review hashes.

Then hide the map and retell the story: why the study was needed, why the design was plausible, which comparisons are decisive, what was seen and what the tested conclusion is. Check a few paper-specific reader questions. Narrative fluency, more words, more nodes or model confidence cannot establish accuracy or reader benefit.
